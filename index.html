Today

Everyone's plans in one place, from a month back to two months ahead. In the Day view a task with a time is a block as tall as its length. Press + or an empty spot to add an appointment; it goes into your plan for that day too. Your own blocks: drag one to another time, drag its bottom edge to change the length, or press it and type both. In the Week view, drag one of your tasks onto another day to move it. On a phone, drag by the six dots. Pressing someone else's task opens their plan. To copy tasks, press Select, press the tasks you want (yours or a buddy's), press Copy, then paste them into any day from today on.
